# Operational Scale

The figures on this page are selected because they provide concrete evidence that Adara is a real production system rather than a design exercise.

## Evidence at a glance

| Dimension | Public evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Production operation | Since June 2023 |
| Production orders | 5,000+ |
| Aggregate traded volume | €18M+ |
| Retained tick corpus | 120M+ records over one nine-month period |
| Product breadth | 50+ operational capabilities |
| Private source history | Five years, 538 commits as of September 2026 |

## Production duration

Adara has operated in production since June 2023 as internal tooling in a real investment-management environment.

That duration demonstrates sustained real use. It is not converted into an unsupported platform-wide uptime percentage.

## Order-processing scale

Adara has processed more than 5,000 production orders.

The figure includes production order activity retained by the platform and is intentionally presented as an aggregate. It does not imply that every historical order originated inside Adara; external exchange activity can also be synchronized and retained with distinct provenance.

## Aggregate traded volume

More than **€18 million** in aggregate traded volume has passed through Adara-supported workflows.

The figure is included as evidence of operational scale, not as evidence of profitability or investment performance.

## Tick-data scale

A retained market-data corpus covering approximately **nine months** contains more than **120 million tick-level observations**.

This is an especially useful engineering metric because it demonstrates the scale of data produced by Adara's tick-driven architecture.

The figure is intentionally narrow and defensible: it describes one retained corpus. It is not presented as a lifetime count of all real-time price updates processed by the system.

## Capability breadth

Adara contains more than 50 operational capabilities across:

- Market Data
- Portfolio
- Trading
- Orders
- Strategies
- Compliance
- Analysis & Reporting
- Administration & Operations

The number describes product responsibilities, not Java classes, endpoints, menu items, database tables, or deployable components.

## Source-code history

The proprietary application repository remains private.

As of September 2026, its development history spans approximately **five years and 538 commits**.

The public case-study repository is deliberately newer and contains documentation rather than a synthetic recreation of the application's historical Git timeline.

## What these figures do and do not establish

Together they establish:

- multi-year production use;
- repeated order processing;
- financially material operational volume;
- sustained tick-data handling;
- broad product scope; and
- a multi-year private engineering history.

They do **not** establish investment returns, profitability, formal regulatory status, system capacity limits, or a global availability percentage.

## Public boundary

Real individual transactions, account identifiers, portfolio values, holdings, investment outcomes, and exact private counters beyond the aggregates disclosed here remain confidential.
