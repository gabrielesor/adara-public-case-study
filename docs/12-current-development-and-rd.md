# Current Development and R&D

This page covers work in progress and exploratory R&D. It is intentionally separated from the production capabilities documented elsewhere in this case study.

## EMIR-oriented reporting evolution

Adara is being extended with an EMIR-oriented regulatory reporting capability that builds on the platform's existing compliance, auditability, and decision-provenance foundations.

The work focuses on maintaining canonical lifecycle records, controlled event mapping, schema-validated report generation, versioned transformations, traceable correction handling, and reproducible audit evidence.

This capability is not currently in production. Regulatory interpretation and validation remain outside the software layer and must be completed before production activation.

## IntentMesh — exploratory R&D

Adara is also one of the practical contexts being considered in the exploratory IntentMesh work around intent-based execution and agent-to-agent collaboration models.

This work is at an early experimental stage and is maintained separately as a public project: [IntentMesh](https://github.com/gabrielesor/intentmesh).

IntentMesh is not currently integrated into production Adara or committed as an Adara V2 production component. The experimental work is not production-ready.
